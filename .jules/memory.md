## Archived: 2026-10-31

- Repository: NopSCADlib
- Description: An ever expanding library of parts modelled in OpenSCAD useful for 3D printers and enclosures for electronics.
- Contains lots of vitamins (non-printed parts), general purpose printed parts, and utilities.
- Includes Python scripts to generate Bills of Materials (BOMs), STL files, DXF files, and assembly instructions.
